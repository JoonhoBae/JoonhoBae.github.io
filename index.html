Welcome! I am a Ph.D. candidate in Technology and Operations at the University of Michigan's Ross School of Business. I am co-advised by John Silberholz and Roman Kapuscinski.

The central aim of my research is the development of mathematical approaches to understand, monitor, and control complex dynamical systems. Motivated by a multidisciplinary background in statistics, financial economics, and industrial engineering, I made on the topic of operations management and management science, especially regarding energy markets. My current research focuses on establishing generalizable lessons on optimal control of battery energy storage systems using analytical modeling frameworks.

Before coming to Michigan, I received my B.S. in Statistics and Financial Economics from Seoul National University (SNU) in 2016 and M.S. in Industrial & Systems Engineering from Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST) in 2019. I also have several working experiences that shaped my research interests in operations management. I co-founded and led a start-up for blind masseurs (Cham Son Gil Cooperative), which recently opened its 8th branch in South Korea. After graduating SNU, I spent a year working at SK Telecom as a full-time marketing manager.
